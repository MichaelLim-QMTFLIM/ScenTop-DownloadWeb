About us

A better way to discover what you smell like

ScenTop started as a small mobile app for people who love fragrance and hate guessing. It's not easy to see what a fragrance will smell like until you try it, so we focus on what people actually notice: the top notes, the mood, and what is popular right now.

This website has one job. It gets you to the app. Everything else, including your account and the full product catalog, lives there now that's your job!.

Top notes first

Every product is described by what you smell in the first minute, so you know what to expect.

See what's trending

The hot list refreshes in the app, so you can follow what people are choosing now.

Built for mobile

ScenTop is an Android app made with Ionic and Angular, and designed for one hand purchase.

FAQ

Questions people ask before downloading

Is ScenTop free?

Yes. Downloading and installing the app costs nothing.

How do I download from a link someone sent me?

Open the link on your phone. It takes you to the Download page, where the button for your device is highlighted. Tap it to continue to the store.

Which devices does it run on?

ScenTop is currently available for Android phones only, as a direct download. It is not on the App Store or Google Play. On a computer, scan the QR code on the Download page with your phone.

Can I buy or sign in on this website?

No. This site only helps you get the app. Accounts and everything else are inside the app.

Where can I see more products?

The carousel on the Home page shows a few of the hottest picks. Tap "See more" to download the app and browse the rest.

The download didn't start. What should I do?

Check that your phone is online and tap the download button again. You can also copy the link on the Download page and open it in your phone's browser. If Android blocks the install, allow installs from your browser in Settings.

How do I contact support?

Write to support@scentop.example. This is a placeholder address for the preview.
